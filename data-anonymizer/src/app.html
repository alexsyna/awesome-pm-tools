3 · Configure column details

Less detail makes people harder to single out. For age and year of diagnosis, “Shown value” sets how wide the ranges are, and “Who's included” limits the file to part of the range.

Profile columns are things others could know (a neighbour, employer, social media), so the tool checks that no combination of them singles anyone out. Survey answers are private: the tool checks they can't be guessed from a person's group.
